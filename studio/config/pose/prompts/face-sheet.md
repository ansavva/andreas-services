A reference sheet of head orientations, laid out as one row of four separate
figures against a flat dark charcoal background, evenly spaced with a clear gap
of empty background between each figure and no overlap.

Every figure is the SAME anonymous artist's head-and-neck mannequin: a smooth,
untextured, matte light-grey sculpt with no hair, no eyebrows, no eyelashes and
no skin detail — an anatomical study of head structure, lit by a soft even
frontal light with gentle shading that makes the planes of the skull, brow, nose
and jaw read clearly. Neutral expression, mouth closed. Identical head, identical
scale and identical lighting in all four; only the rotation changes.

Left to right, the four orientations:

1. Front on, squared to camera, facing the lens.
2. Rotated 45 degrees so the face points toward frame right — the three-quarter
   view showing one cheek and the far brow.
3. Full profile, rotated 90 degrees so the face points directly toward frame
   right, the whole nose and jaw line in silhouette against the background.
4. Chin lowered slightly, front on, facing the lens.

No text, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no arrows, no grid lines, no
watermark. No clothing, no shoulders beyond the base of the neck, no props. Flat
even background with no gradient, no vignette and no floor line.
