A reference sheet of standing body orientations, laid out as one row of five
separate full-length figures against a flat dark charcoal background, evenly
spaced with a clear gap of empty background between each figure and no overlap.
Every figure is complete from the top of the head to the soles of the feet.

Every figure is the SAME anonymous artist's anatomy mannequin: a smooth,
untextured, matte light-grey sculpt of an adult human body with no hair, no face
detail and no skin texture — an anatomical study of proportion and musculature,
lit by a soft even frontal light with gentle shading that makes the muscle groups
and the line of the limbs read clearly. Standing upright, weight even on both
legs, arms relaxed at the sides and clear of the body. Identical figure,
identical scale and identical lighting in all five; only the rotation changes.

Left to right, the five orientations:

1. Front on, squared to camera.
2. Rotated 45 degrees so the body and head face toward frame right — the
   three-quarter view.
3. Full profile, rotated 90 degrees facing frame right.
4. Rotated 135 degrees — the rear three-quarter, seen mostly from behind.
5. Straight from behind, squared to camera.

No text, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no arrows, no grid lines, no
watermark. No clothing and no props. Flat even background with no gradient, no
vignette and no floor line, though a soft contact shadow under each foot is fine.
